#1.1. Connections

!!! definition "Definition 1.1.1"

    A (*Koszul*) *connection* on a $\bbk$-vector bundle $E$ is a $\bbk$-linear map $\nabla\: \Gamma(E) \to \Gamma(T^\vee \otimes_\bbR E)$ satisfying the Leibniz rule
        
    \[ \nabla(fs) = df \otimes s + f \,\nabla s\]
        
    for all $f \in C^\infty(M; \bbk)$ and $s \in \Gamma(E)$.

    We denote the set of all connections on $E$ by $\Conn(E)$.

Given connection $\nabla \in \Conn(E)$, a vector field $X \in \frakX(M)$ and a section $s \in \Gamma(E)$, we denote the image of $s$ under the composition
<script type="text/tikz">
\newcommand{\bbR}{\mathbb{R}}
\newcommand{\ev}{\mathrm{ev}}
\begin{tikzcd}[column sep=normal]
    \Gamma(E)
        \arrow[r, "\nabla"]
    & \Gamma(T^\vee M \otimes_\bbR E)
        \arrow[r, "{\langle {-}, X \rangle_{\ev} \otimes_{\bbR} {\id}}"]
    &[3em] \Gamma(E)
\end{tikzcd}
</script>
by $\nablax{X} s$. Then, $\nablax{X}\: \Gamma(E) \to \Gamma(E)$ becomes a $\bbk$-linear endomorphism. In terms of the map $\nablax{X}$, the Leibniz rule is written as

\[ \nabla_X(fs) = Xf \cdot s + f\,\nablax{X}s \text. \]
